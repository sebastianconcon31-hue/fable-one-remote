How to fly

You start on the pad with both engines running and the rotor turning.

Take off

  1. Hold R to raise the collective until you lift off (about 55%).
  2. W A S D tilt the cyclic, Q E press the pedals.
  3. F lowers the collective to land.

Shut down and start up

  1. Pull both power levers (left console) back to OFF. Click a lever to move it; right-click moves it back.
  2. Set the rotor brake to BRK once the rotor slows.
  3. Press APU. When it's lit, lever to IDLE, flip ENG 1 START up, then ENG 2.
  4. Brake OFF, levers to FLY.

Weapons

  • Press ARM/SAFE to arm and GND ORIDE to fire on the ground.
  • G or the WPN page picks gun, rockets or missiles. Space fires.
  • In the gunner's seat, I J K L slew the sight. The left grip's trigger fires the laser.

Displays

  • The keys under each display change pages. The keys round the edge pick options.
  • Type on the keyboard units. The up-front display's RTS key changes radio.

Keys

  • 1 2 3 change seats. The arrow keys and dragging empty space look around; scroll to zoom.
  • Scroll over a knob to turn it.
  • In VR, point and pull the trigger, or grab sticks and levers with the grip. The right thumbstick flies the cyclic, the left the collective and pedals.
